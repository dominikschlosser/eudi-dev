A minimal OpenID4VP verifier for demo purposes

This verifier requests a presentation through a standard OpenID4VP authorization request (dcql_query, direct_post) and cryptographically verifies the response: issuer signature against the wallet's trust anchor, key binding, sd_hash, nonce, and audience.
